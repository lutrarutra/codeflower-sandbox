Audit how much of a published paper reproduces. Follow the codeflower-audit skill.

The paper is the one `codeflower-doctor` names under "Paper audit", or the one in `audit/paper.yaml` if this workspace already has an audit; otherwise ask me for its DOI (or a PDF) in one message before anything else.

Work through the skill's steps. Stop at its two checkpoints: before building anything, show me the results you will check (and which are key), what each needs to reproduce from raw data compared with what this sandbox has, and ask me how deep to go (from raw data, from processed data, or from the authors' results); and go through your outcomes with me before scoring. Never write a score yourself, and do not change an outcome because I want a better one: only for a reason you accept, recorded. Before sealing, tell me that the sealed folder, including this chat's transcript, will be public.

At the end tell me in a few lines: the headline, where the sealed folder is on my computer, and how to push it unchanged to GitHub.
